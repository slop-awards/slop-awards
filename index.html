About the Awards

The Slop Academy Awards are an evening for celebrating the side projects our colleagues build with AI: the hobby apps, home gadgets, odd little games and weekend experiments that never make it into a work meeting. Bring yours, show everyone how you made it, and go home with a few new tricks (and maybe a prize).

Plenty of us already use AI on the quiet, in the evenings and at weekends. This is shadow AI at its most harmless and most fun, and it deserves a stage. So we're bringing it into the light: AI-built projects, proudly owned as such.

Why "slop"? Because you let the AI take the wheel. You described what you wanted, it did the building, and something genuinely cool came out the other end, whether or not you fully understand how. That's not something to hide. It's the whole point of the evening.

On Monday, 7 December 2026, in the ABL Auditorium, each presenter gets the stage for ten minutes and a jury hands out real prizes. There are only a few house rules:

  1. IRule I Make It for Fun

    Your project should come from your own curiosity, not your day job or your research. Games, gadgets, home automations, tools for your club, kitchen or garden: if you built it because you wanted it to exist, it counts.

  2. IIRule II Let the AI Drive

    AI should have done most of the work, as pair programmer, designer, or the entire engineering department. You don't need to understand every line it wrote, or indeed any of them. Clever beats expensive: we are far more impressed by what you got out of a modest setup than by the size of your token bill.

  3. IIIRule III Own It

    Present your project like it matters, because it does. Own the fact that AI built it, too. It's just a silly side project is banned from the stage. Tell us what it does, why you wanted it, and what surprised you along the way.

  4. IVRule IV Ten Minutes of Fame

    Each presenter gets ten minutes, including a few for questions from the audience. Diagrams, war stories, lessons learned and wildly ambitious roadmaps are all very welcome.

  5. VRule V Demos Welcome

    Live demos are encouraged. Live demos are also live. We will cheer either way.

  6. VIRule VI Show Your Tools

    Tell us which AI models and tools you used. The prompts that worked, the ones that backfired, and the best hallucinations you ran into make great material, and they are how the rest of us learn.
