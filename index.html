The Proclamation

Dear Esteemed Colleagues, Distinguished Scholars,

It is with immense pride, considerable solemnity, and a sense of historical responsibility that we announce the First Annual Slop Academy Awards, an occasion dedicated to recognising the most extraordinary achievements in the increasingly distinguished field of Artificially Generated Nonsense.

On Tuesday, 15 December 2026, the doors of the ABL Auditorium shall open to an assembly of exceptional minds, gathered to celebrate projects of remarkable technical ambition, dubious practical utility, and entirely disproportionate self-importance. A distinguished jury shall deliberate upon the merits of each contribution. Actual prizes shall be awarded by the Academy to submissions in accordance with the following immutable principles:

  1. IArticle I The Principle of Glorious Irrelevance

    All projects must be entirely unrelated to one's professional duties, principal research endeavours, or any undertaking of demonstrable societal importance.

  2. IIArticle II The Artificial Intelligence Mandate

    Submissions must have been substantially generated through the assistance of artificial intelligence. Excessive reliance upon one's own programming abilities, whilst not expressly forbidden, is regarded with some suspicion.

  3. IIIArticle III The Requirement of Scholarly Gravitas

    All participants shall present their work with the utmost academic seriousness, irrespective of how profoundly ridiculous their creation may be. The words it's just a stupid side project shall not constitute an acceptable scientific justification.

  4. IVArticle IV The Defence of One's Contribution

    Each nominee shall be granted ten minutes in which to present their groundbreaking contribution, the final portion of which shall be devoted to rigorous scholarly interrogation. The use of elaborate diagrams, exaggerated performance claims, unnecessarily complex architectures, and references to future work is strongly encouraged.

  5. VArticle V The Demonstration of Excellence

    Live demonstrations are encouraged. Their successful execution is, naturally, not guaranteed.

  6. VIArticle VI The Declaration of Artificial Assistance

    Candidates shall disclose the AI models employed in the creation of their masterpiece. Particularly shameful prompts, inexplicable architectural decisions, and remarkable hallucinations may be submitted as supplementary evidence.
